Privacy Policy

Skyline Stacker · Effective date: October 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Miloš Mitrović, doing business as Tiny Tap Games ("we", "us", "our"), handles personal information in connection with the mobile game Skyline Stacker (the "Game"). We are the party responsible for the processing described here (the "controller"). Contact details are in section 15.

The short version

  • The Game has no accounts and no sign-in. Your progress (best score, coins, items, settings) is stored only on your device. We never receive it.
  • The Game shows optional rewarded video ads from Google AdMob. Google collects device and usage information (such as your advertising ID and IP address) to show ads and prevent fraud. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, personalized ads are shown only with your consent.
  • If you email us, we receive your email address and message, and use them only to reply.
  • We do not use analytics, do not ask for your location, contacts, camera or microphone, and do not sell your personal information for money.
  • You can delete your game data at any time with Settings → Reset progress, or by uninstalling the Game.

1. What information is collected

Game data stored on your device (not collected by us)

Your best score, coins, unlocked and equipped items, sound and vibration settings, and a random identifier used only to keep your save data consistent are stored in the Game's storage on your device. This data is not sent to us or to any server we operate. If Android's backup feature is turned on, Android may back up this data to your Google account under Google's own privacy policy.

Information you give us

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address, the contents of your message, and anything else you choose to include (for example, your name if you sign the email).

Information collected by Google AdMob

The Game uses Google AdMob, Google's mobile advertising service, to show optional rewarded video ads. When the Game requests or shows an ad, Google automatically collects information from your device, which may include:

  • Device identifiers, such as your device's advertising ID;
  • IP address, which Google may use to estimate your approximate location (for example, country or city);
  • Device information, such as device model, operating system and version, language, and mobile carrier;
  • Ad interactions and diagnostics, such as which ads were shown, whether you watched or tapped them, and technical or crash information related to ad delivery.

We do not receive this information about individual players. We only see aggregated reports, such as the total number of ads shown and our earnings. Google's use of this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

What we do not collect

The Game does not ask for or collect your name, phone number, postal address, payment information, precise (GPS) location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or any other device features. The Game has no analytics, no user accounts, and no chat or user-generated content.

3. Who information is shared with

  • Google (AdMob). Google collects the information described in section 1 directly through the AdMob software in the Game, and acts as an independent controller of that information under the Google Ads Controller-Controller Data Protection Terms and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Our email provider. Support emails are stored by our email provider, Google (Gmail), which processes them on our behalf.
  • Authorities. We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect our rights or the safety of others.
  • Business transfers. If the Game or our business is sold or transferred, information may be transferred to the new owner, who will be bound by this policy.

We do not sell personal information for money. Under some US state laws, allowing Google to collect information for personalized advertising may count as "selling" or "sharing" personal information, or as "targeted advertising". See section 11 for how to opt out.

4. Advertising and your choices

All ads in the Game are optional. They only play when you choose to watch one, for example to continue a run or to double your coins.

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: before personalized ads are shown, the Game asks for your consent using Google's consent form. If you do not consent, you may still see ads, but they will not be personalized: either non-personalized ads, which are based on context and use limited information for measurement and fraud prevention, or Google's "limited ads", which do not use cookies or device identifiers for advertising. Sometimes no ad may be available.
  • Changing your choice: you can review or change your consent at any time in the Game under Settings → Privacy & ad choices.
  • On your device: on Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in your device's settings (usually Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Google's controls: you can manage ad personalization at My Ad Center.

5. Device storage and tracking technologies

The Game stores your progress and settings in your device's local storage. This is strictly necessary for the Game to work and is not used for tracking.

Google AdMob uses your device's advertising ID and similar technologies to deliver and measure ads. Ads may also contain web content that uses cookies or pixels. These technologies are controlled by Google and are covered by the choices in section 4.

Some browsers and devices offer a "Do Not Track" signal. There is no common standard for responding to it, so the Game does not respond to such signals. Use the choices in section 4 instead.

6. International transfers

We are based in Serbia. If you email us, your message is transferred to us in Serbia and stored by our email provider, whose servers may be in the United States and other countries. We transfer it because you chose to contact us and we need it to reply.

Google processes advertising information in the United States and other countries. For transfers of personal information from the EEA, UK and Switzerland, Google relies on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and on its certification under the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework (and its UK and Swiss extensions). These countries may not have data protection laws as comprehensive as those in your country, but these safeguards are designed to protect your information to the standard required by European law.

7. How long information is kept

  • Support emails: until your request is resolved, and then for up to 1 year in case of follow-up questions, after which we delete them.
  • Game data on your device: until you use Settings → Reset progress or uninstall the Game. We never hold it.
  • Advertising information: kept by Google according to Google's retention policies. We do not store it.

We may keep information longer only if the law requires it.

8. Security

The only personal information we hold is support email, which we keep in an email account protected by a strong password and two-factor authentication. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a data breach affects your personal information, we will notify you and the relevant authorities where the law requires.

9. Children

The Game is intended for players aged 16 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16 (or under 13 in the United States). If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please email us at smallgamedev02@gmail.com and we will delete it.
